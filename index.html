01 — The Case

You've earned
the customers.
Now own them.

Burger Mafia at Hoodi Main Road holds a 4.9 rating across 71 reviews. That is not a marketing problem — that is a kitchen doing its job exceptionally well. The problem is who currently owns the relationship with everyone who tastes it.

Today a hungry customer in Hoodi opens an aggregator app, and Burger Mafia appears beside twenty competitors, in their layout, under their search ranking, paying their commission on every order. You supply the food and the reputation. They keep the customer, the data and a cut of the bill.

This proposal is about changing that — while matching, feature for feature, the ordering platform of the biggest burger chain in the country.

18–25%Typical aggregator commission on every order they send you

WHY NOW 01

Commission compounds

Every order routed through an aggregator gives away a slice of a margin that is already thin in fast food. A direct order carries a payment fee of roughly two percent instead. The platform doesn't need to replace the aggregators — shifting even a modest share of repeat customers to direct ordering changes the economics of the outlet.

WHY NOW 02

You can't market to people you don't know

You cannot text a discount, launch a new burger to your regulars, or win back someone who stopped ordering, because you do not hold their number. Your own platform builds that list from the first order — and it belongs to you permanently.

WHY NOW 03

Your regulars are your cheapest growth

A 4.9 rating means people come back. A loyalty programme turns that goodwill into measurable repeat frequency — and on your own platform it costs you points, not commission.

We are not proposing you leave Zomato and Swiggy. Keep them for discovery — that is what they are good at. Own the second order, and every one after it.

Opportunity

Delivery and takeaway only. A guest sitting at one of their tables gets nothing from the platform — no menu, no ordering, no loyalty accrual, no data captured.

Opportunity

03 — The Platform

What we
will build

Two products, one system. A guest-facing ordering experience, and the back office that runs your restaurant — because with no till to plug into, our admin becomes your point of sale.

MODULE 01

Sign-in built for Indian customers

Mobile number and OTP — no passwords to forget. Guests browse the full menu freely and only identify themselves at checkout, so nothing stands between hunger and the basket.

  • Phone OTP login and registration
  • Guest browsing with no barrier
  • Secure sessions that survive a returning visit
MODULE 02

Location, mode and availability

The guest tells us where they are and how they want to eat; the platform decides what it can honestly sell them. No taking an order the kitchen can't fulfil.

  • Delivery · Takeaway · Dine-in mode switch
  • Address autocomplete and GPS detection
  • Delivery radius enforcement with clear messaging
  • Saved addresses labelled home, work or other
  • Store page with live open and closed state
MODULE 03

A menu that sells

The commercial heart of the platform, built for your actual range — burgers, pizza, fries, sides and desserts, each of which needs a different way of ordering.

  • Merchandised home feed with banners and top deals
  • Categories, veg and non-veg filtering, live search
  • Burger customiser — add, remove, upgrade, choose size
  • Pizza builder — crust, size, toppings, half-and-half
  • Combo and meal builder across burgers, sides and drinks
  • Product pages with nutrition and allergen information
  • Upsell prompts at add-to-cart and at basket
  • Honest sold-out states, never a dead end
MODULE 04

Basket and checkout

The shortest credible path from craving to confirmed order, with every rupee on screen accounted for before the guest commits.

  • Server-side basket that follows the customer across devices
  • Coupon entry with instant, honest validation
  • Transparent bill — items, packaging, delivery, taxes, discount
  • Tip the rider, and round up to donate
  • Kitchen notes and special instructions
MODULE 05

Payments, the way India pays

One gateway covering every method your customers already use — plus cash, and proper recovery when a payment stalls halfway.

  • UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets
  • Cash on delivery
  • Automatic reconciliation of interrupted payments
  • Card details never touch our servers
MODULE 06

Live order tracking

The window where most brands lose trust. Status moves in real time, and the updates reach the guest where they'll actually read them.

  • Live stages from accepted through to delivered
  • Updates by WhatsApp and browser notification
  • Order history with genuine one-tap reorder
  • Cancellation inside a fair window
  • Star and per-item feedback capture after every order
MODULE 07

Dine-in by QR code

A guest at your table scans, browses, orders and pays without waiting for anyone. The incumbent offers nothing here — this is where Burger Mafia visibly leads.

  • Unique QR per table, no app download
  • Table session with a shared, running basket

  • Dine-in guests earn loyalty like everyone else

05 — Design & Brand

We build the
whole identity

The kitchen has a reputation; the brand doesn't yet have a system. There is no logo standard, no palette, no photography library and nothing that looks the same twice across a menu board, a delivery box and a listing photo. We build all of it — identity, food photography and platform — as one coherent piece of work.

DELIVERABLE 01

Brand identity

Name lockup and logo, colour palette, typography, tone of voice and a brand book your team can hold suppliers to. Extended to packaging, signage and in-store mockups.

DELIVERABLE 02

Food photography

Art-directed studio shoot with a food stylist, covering every menu item plus hero imagery for the home page and campaigns. In a food business, the photography is the product page.

DELIVERABLE 03

Design system

Every screen designed and signed off before a line of production code is written. Two creative directions to choose from, then one refined to completion — storefront, dine-in and back office.

The look we're aiming for

Dark, confident and high contrast. Oversized display type, one loud accent colour, and food photography given room to do the selling. Closer to a premium streetwear drop than a fast-food menu board.

How it should feel

Mobile first and thumb friendly, with motion used to give feedback rather than decorate. Items fly into a sticky basket; sheets slide up instead of modals blocking the view; everything holds a smooth frame rate on a mid-range Android phone.

09 — Straight Talk

Where this
could go wrong

We would rather raise these now than discover them together in week fourteen.

RISK 01

We become your till

With no existing point of sale, if our back office goes down, your counter stops taking orders. That is a serious responsibility and we treat it as one.

  • Kitchen screen keeps working through a network drop
  • Printed ticket fallback for the line
  • Monitoring and alerting from day one
  • A support commitment with defined response times
RISK 02

Photography gates the design

Menu design can't be finished around placeholder images. If the shoot slips, the build waits — so the shoot is booked in week two, before design begins in earnest.

RISK 03

The name is crowded

Other unrelated businesses already trade as Burger Mafia in Pune, Amritsar and Kerala. That is workable locally, but exclusive national rights to the name are unlikely, so we would not build a growth plan that depends on owning it. Worth a trademark search before we finalise the mark — and worth deciding now whether the identity leans on a distinctive lockup you can protect. Separately: we copy the incumbent's capability, never their name, logo or imagery.
