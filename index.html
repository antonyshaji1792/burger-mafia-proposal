01 — Overview

What we
build

Your own direct ordering channel. Customers order and pay on your website; your staff manage the menu and orders from one admin panel. No aggregator commission on direct orders — only the payment gateway fee of about 2%.

01

Ordering website

Mobile-first web app. Menu, item options, basket and checkout for delivery and takeaway. No app download needed.

02

Online payment

Razorpay for UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets, plus cash on delivery. Payments are verified server-side before an order is confirmed.

03

Admin panel

Manage items, prices, photos and availability. Receive orders in real time, accept or reject, and update status. It also works as your till.

Payment flow
  1. Customer confirms the basket. The API re-prices every line from the database — the browser never sets the price.
  2. API creates a pending order and a matching Razorpay order for the exact amount.
  3. Customer pays in Razorpay Checkout (UPI, card, netbanking or wallet). Card data never touches our servers.
  4. Razorpay webhook arrives. The signature is verified with the secret key; only then is the order marked paid.
  5. Admin panel is alerted instantly over the socket, with a sound, and the customer sees the confirmation page.
  6. A reconciliation job runs every few minutes to settle payments where the customer closed the tab mid-flow.

07 — Risks

Things to
plan for

RISK 01

The admin panel is your till

If it goes down, the counter stops taking orders. Mitigated with uptime monitoring, a printed-ticket fallback and a support agreement with defined response times.

RISK 02

Stock images aren’t your food

Royalty-free images fill gaps but won’t show your actual burgers. Send your own photos early — they can be swapped in the admin panel any time.

RISK 03

Late menu content

The build is driven by the menu structure. If items, prices and options arrive late, the launch date moves with them.
