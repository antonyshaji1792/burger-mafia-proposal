01 — Overview

What we
build

Your own direct ordering channel. Customers order and pay on your website; your staff manage the menu and orders from one admin panel. No aggregator commission on direct orders — only the payment gateway fee of about 2%.

01

Ordering website

Mobile-first web app. Menu, item options, basket and checkout for delivery and takeaway. No app download needed.

02

Online payment

Razorpay for UPI, cards, netbanking and wallets, plus cash on delivery. Payments are verified server-side before an order is confirmed.

03

Admin panel

Manage items, prices, photos and availability. Receive orders in real time, accept or reject, and update status. It also works as your till.

Payment flow
  1. Customer confirms the basket. The server re-prices every line from the database — the browser never sets the price.
  2. Laravel creates a pending order and a matching Razorpay order for the exact amount.
  3. Customer pays in Razorpay Checkout (UPI, card, netbanking or wallet). Card data never touches our server.
  4. Razorpay webhook arrives. The signature is verified with the secret key; only then is the order marked paid.
  5. Admin panel is alerted instantly through Reverb, with a sound, and the customer sees the confirmation page.
  6. A scheduled job runs every few minutes to settle payments where the customer closed the tab mid-flow.
